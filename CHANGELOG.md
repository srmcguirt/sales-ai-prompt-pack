# Changelog

All notable changes to the Sales AI Prompt Pack are documented here.

## [1.0.0] - 2026-08-11

### Added
- `01-prospect-research-analyst.md` — Account intelligence brief with ICP fit, trigger events, stakeholder map, outreach frame, and research gaps
- `02-cold-email-writer.md` — 3-variant cold email generator with subject lines, personalization guardrails, and send sequence
- `03-discovery-call-prep.md` — Complete discovery prep with hypothesis, question bank by category, objection handlers, and MEDDIC qualification checkpoint
- `04-demo-script-builder.md` — Persona-tailored 3-act demo script with transitions, check-ins, and post-demo follow-up template
- `05-objection-handler.md` — Objection decoder with underlying concern analysis, clarifying questions, and exact response language
- `06-proposal-writer.md` — Full proposal document generator with executive summary, ROI business case, implementation plan, and next steps
- `07-win-loss-analyzer.md` — Deal pattern analysis with competitive intelligence, buyer interview synthesis, and actionable recommendations
- `08-followup-sequence-writer.md` — 6-email post-meeting sequence with timing, hooks, stakeholder multi-thread, and break-up email
- Few-shot examples for all 8 prompts (3 examples each)
- JSON output schemas for all 8 prompts
